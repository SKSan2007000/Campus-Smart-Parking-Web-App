PROPOSED REAL-WORLD FLOW

Designed for a campus, not just a parking lot.

01

Slow & capture

A speed table encourages low speed near a number-plate camera. Pre-registered students and faculty need not stop at a manual gate; failed recognition goes to an attendant.

02

Assign & notify

Check pass status, vehicle type, EV charging request and reserved slots; record entry time and assigned location.

03

Scan to find

The QR opens a ticket snapshot with its assigned floor, slot, entry time and lane. Slot location is highlighted in the demo map.

04

Exit & calculate

At exit, retrieve the active session, calculate duration and separate charging fees, then release the slot.
